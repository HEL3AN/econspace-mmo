Full-screen passes for the screen treatment (#120).

Every one of these is handed the same four uniforms, and uses the ones it has a use for:

  amount      0..1, how much of the effect. Zero is off; one is the effect as designed.
  scale       the pass's own second knob -- glow reach, pixel size, line pitch. What it
              means is written at the top of each file and shown in the settings screen.
  resolution  the render target in pixels.
  time        seconds, for anything that moves.

raylib supplies the vertex stage and binds the incoming image as `texture0`, so these are
fragment shaders only. GLSL 330 -- the desktop backend. A shader that will not compile on
a particular driver is dropped and reported; the game keeps playing without that pass.
